# Design system — Agritech & Fertilizer Landing Page Template

`SN-W00022` · ai-saas · light mode · Sinera Atlas theme `circuit` · language `en`

## Direction

Clean clinical agritech stance: a white and pale slate ground with Inter throughout, where green is the only colour. The emerald (#10b981) marks the key phrase in the headline and the primary button, while the secondary button stays outlined. Hierarchy comes from the weight and size of the 60px headline rather than decoration.

**Mood:** clean, clinical, trustworthy, minimal, green

## Colour

| Role | Value | Use |

| Background | `#f8fafc` | Page ground |
| Surface | `#ffffff` | Cards, panels, raised areas |
| Text | `#1e293b` | Headings and primary copy |
| Muted text | `#64748b` | Secondary copy, captions |
| Border | `#ffffff` | Hairlines, dividers, outlines |

Palette by frequency of use: `#ffffff` · `#10b981` · `#0f172a` · `#64748b` · `#e2e8f0` · `#059669` · `#475569` · `#047857` · `#34d399` · `#94a3b8`.

Roles are inferred from how often and where each colour is used; check them against the page before relying on them.

## Do and don't

- **Do:** Colour the key phrase of the headline in #10b981 and nothing else in the headline
- **Do:** Use the emerald (#10b981) for the primary button only, with an outlined button as the secondary action
- **Do:** Keep all text in Inter and rely on weight and size for hierarchy
- **Don't:** Don't add a second accent colour; the page is green on white only
- **Don't:** Don't use dark bands; the page ground stays pale (#f8fafc)
- **Don't:** Don't mix extra corner radii; buttons are rounded-full and cards rounded-lg
