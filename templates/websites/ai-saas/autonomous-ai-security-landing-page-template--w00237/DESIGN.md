# Design system — Autonomous AI Security Landing Page Template

`SN-W00237` · ai-saas · dark mode · Sinera Atlas theme `circuit` · language `en`

## Direction

A near-black hero with a two-tone headline, white on 'Build with' and grey on 'Autonomous AI', set left-aligned above a short paragraph. On the right, a thin dotted orbit ring and a diamond chip show the system as a diagram, with small nodes in violet and rose around it. The buttons are white filled and dark outlined, and small monospace badges sit under them.

**Mood:** dark, diagrammatic, precise, quiet

## Do and don't

- **Do:** Set the headline in two tones: white for the first line, grey for the second
- **Do:** Use the orbit diagram as the hero visual, drawn with thin strokes and small nodes
- **Do:** Keep the monospace compliance badges small and under the buttons
- **Don't:** Don't fill the orbit or the nodes with solid colours
- **Don't:** Don't centre the headline; it is left-aligned
- **Don't:** Don't add a second background colour to the hero
