Why organic

Three growers' worries, answered plainly.

"Won't plants grow slower than with chemical fertilizer?"

Organic fertilizer releases nutrients gradually, so there is no sudden flush. In return the soil keeps its nutrients and water, and plants stay steady all season instead of spiking and fading.

"Is it safe for leafy vegetables?"

The fertilizer is fully composted and tested batch by batch: [YOUR TEST PARAMETERS]. Apply it to the soil, not onto the leaves, and stop [NUMBER] days before harvest.

Ask before you order

Does it smell?

Fully composted fertilizer has only a light earthy smell. If you notice a strong sharp odor, tell us on [CONTACT CHANNEL] and we will check the batch.

When is the best time to apply it?

Ideally before sowing or at the start of the season, while the soil is still moist. Avoid the harsh midday sun and water lightly afterwards.

Can I use it alongside chemical fertilizer?

Yes. Many gardens cut back on chemical fertilizer as the soil improves. Reduce step by step and watch the plants rather than stopping completely in a single season.

How should I store it, and how long does it keep?
