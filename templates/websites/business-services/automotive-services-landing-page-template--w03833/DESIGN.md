# Design system — Automotive Services Landing Page Template

`SN-W03833` · business-services · dark mode · Sinera Atlas theme `route` · language `cs`

## Direction

A black hero with a large uppercase headline in white, whose second line is set in lime (#bbcf1d). A glowing lime primary button sits beside an outline button, with two short statistics beneath them. The navigation is an outlined pill with a lime contact button, so the lime repeats at the top and in the main call to action.

**Mood:** bold, polished, high-contrast, protective

## Do and don't

- **Do:** Set the second headline line in lime (#bbcf1d) in uppercase.
- **Do:** Use the lime for the primary button and the contact button only.
- **Do:** Place the two statistics small and in light grey below the buttons.
- **Don't:** Don't use lime for body copy.
- **Don't:** Don't place a light background behind the hero.
- **Don't:** Don't add a second highlight colour to the headline.
