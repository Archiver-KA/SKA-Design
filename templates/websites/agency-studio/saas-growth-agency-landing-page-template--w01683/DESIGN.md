# Design system — SaaS Growth Agency Landing Page Template

`SN-W01683` · agency-studio · dark mode · Sinera Atlas theme `workshop` · language `en`

## Direction

The same dark family as the roofing template: a near-black ground, white headlines with a coral (#fb7185) italic serif highlight, and a thin outlined label above. The hero uses a bordered dark card for the product demo, and the page is built from stacked dark bands. Colour is limited to the coral highlight and white text.

**Mood:** dark, sober, high-contrast, direct

## Do and don't

- **Do:** Set the highlighted phrase in italic coral (#fb7185) with the serif face.
- **Do:** Keep headlines white on near-black (#0a0a0a).
- **Do:** Frame the product demo in a thin-bordered dark card.
- **Don't:** Don't use the coral highlight for body text or links.
- **Don't:** Don't add a bright hero gradient; depth comes from the dark stacked bands.
- **Don't:** Don't leave the bracket placeholders such as [Key Result] in headlines when publishing.
