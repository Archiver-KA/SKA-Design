Ask before you buy

Can I use it on sensitive skin?

Patch-test on a small area (inner wrist) and wait [NUMBER] hours. If you notice itching, redness or stinging, stop using it and ask a dermatologist. See the ingredients: [INGREDIENT LIST].

Will it streak or turn orange?

Streaks usually come from skin that was not exfoliated or from uneven application. Following the three steps above and using the included mitt greatly reduces the risk.

Does it replace sunscreen?

No. The colour gives no sun protection, so you still need sunscreen when you go outside.
