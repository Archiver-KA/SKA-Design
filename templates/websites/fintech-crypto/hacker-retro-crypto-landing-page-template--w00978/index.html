How it works

Three things you usually take on trust, here you can read.

01 / log

Every reward has a transaction ID

Each cashback line is tied to an original transaction and a hash. Check it against your wallet history, no need to ask support.

02 / keys

Your keys stay with you, not with us

Non-custodial wallet. We only read your public address to calculate rewards. We cannot withdraw or move your assets.

03 / payout

Rewards land in your wallet on a fixed cycle

Payout cycle: [CYCLE, E.G. WEEKLY]. When the cycle ends, rewards are booked and sent straight to your wallet.

Steps

  2. $ link

    Link a card or merchant

  3. $ spend

    Spend as usual

    Check the log, then claim your reward when the cycle ends.

No. The wallet is non-custodial; we only read your public address to calculate cashback.

How do I know a reward is correct?

Every log line carries the original transaction ID so you can check it on the public ledger.
