Start with what your loved one needs.

Support with daily living

Personal care, meals, medication reminders, getting about and companionship. Ideal when your relative is mentally sharp but would benefit from someone alongside them.

Visits: hourly, daily or overnight. [YOUR PACKAGES]

Care after leaving hospital

Our nurses follow the instructions of the treating clinician: monitoring vital signs, changing dressings and guiding basic rehabilitation exercises. We never change a treatment plan ourselves.

Please have to hand: the discharge summary and current prescription.

Support with long-term conditions

Help with checking blood sugar and blood pressure, reminders for appointments and a symptom diary, so the family and GP can see the full picture.

Anything unusual is reported to the family straight away, with guidance on contacting the right health service.

How we choose who comes to your home.

  1. 01

    Talking through needs

    A coordinator asks about medical history, daily routine, language and what the family hopes for.

  2. 02

    Home assessment

    A nurse visits to look at the home, independence levels and falls risk, then writes up a care plan.

  3. 03

    Meeting your carer

    You meet the person before they start. If it isn't the right fit, we change them, no lengthy explanations needed.

  4. 04

    A report after every visit

    Notes on meals, medication and daily activity, sent to the family in the way you prefer.
